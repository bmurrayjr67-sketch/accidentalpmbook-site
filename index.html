The Accidental Project Manager, Book 2

The Busy Work Was the Training

How Project Managers Learn the Job and Keep It in the Age of AI

Open the Field Kit

AI is taking the busy work whether you like it or not: the status updates, the meeting notes, the first drafts of the plan. For years, that work was how project managers learned the job. With that going away you need a plan.

This book is for two kinds of project managers. If you're new, it's about learning the job when the work that used to teach it is gone. If you've been doing this a while, it's about what to do with the time AI gives back, and how to make that time show in your results.

This is one working PM's view, drawn from more than 25 years of running projects and daily use of AI on them in the past year. It covers four things: what to hand off, how to check what comes back, how to own it once it goes out, and what's still worth getting better at.
