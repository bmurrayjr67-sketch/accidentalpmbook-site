"Where the time went" note

Worksheet 9 of 10, from Chapter 10 of the book.

Once a month. For yourself. If your weekly status already shows where your time goes, it may do this job for you.

Month: __________

  1. What the AI took off my plate, and about how many hours each saved: ______________________
  2. Where that time went (more projects, better work, the things I never had time for, meetings and email), and which skill it used (judgment, building relationships, knowing my company, running effective meetings, follow-through). Conversations with colleagues, noted separately from meetings I didn’t need to be in: ______________________
  3. What it produced (something I can point to: a project, a problem I headed off and when I followed up, a plan that’s further along): ______________________
  4. One change for next month: ______________________

Write down where your saved time went, and make sure it went somewhere that shows your worth.
