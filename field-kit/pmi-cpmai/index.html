PMI-CPMAI: the details

Last checked 23 November 2026, on pmi.org. Check PMI's site before you sign up for anything. These details change.

The PMI Certified Professional in Managing AI (PMI-CPMAI) is PMI's credential for managing AI projects. PMI introduced it on 30 September 2025, and it replaced PMI's earlier CPMAI certification. Chapter 11 of the book talks about where credentials fit.

The exam

  • 120 questions.
  • 160 minutes.
  • Offered in English, Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, French, German, Japanese, Korean and Latin American Spanish.

The prep course

  • PMI offers a 21-hour PMI-CPMAI Exam Prep Course, sold together with the certification.
  • Completing it earns 21 PDUs toward your other PMI certifications.
  • PMI also sells an official practice exam (about three hours, 3 PDUs).

Who can take it

  • PMI lists it as "No experience required."

What it costs

  • PMI's pricing depends on your region and whether you're a PMI member. PMI's own pages don't publish a single dollar figure, so check the price for your region on pmi.org.

Keeping it

  • Holders earn 30 professional development units (PDUs) every three years.
