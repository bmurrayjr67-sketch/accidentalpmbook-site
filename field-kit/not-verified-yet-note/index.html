"Not verified yet" note

Worksheet 6 of 10, from Chapter 7 of the book.

Put DRAFT in the subject line. Put this note at the top of the email, above the plan.

  • What this is (a draft, a proposal, or options for discussion): ______________________
  • What’s confirmed, and who agreed: ______________________
  • What isn’t verified yet, and what it’s waiting on: ______________________
  • What it isn’t: Not approved. Not a commitment.
  • What I need back, from whom, by when: ______________________

Don’t treat a date as a commitment until the people doing the work and the person with authority have both agreed to it, in writing.
