Meeting notes template

Worksheet 2 of 10, from Chapter 3 of the book.

A plain version of the standing instructions I give the AI with every recording. Adjust the sections to what your readers expect.

Write meeting notes from this recording, using these sections in this order:

  1. Follow-up tasks and requested work. Each with an owner and a due date.
  2. Risks and open items.
  3. Key updates.
  4. Decisions.
  5. Next steps.

List owners by name. If a section has nothing in it, write “None identified.” Don’t make up an owner or a due date that nobody actually said.

Fix these names, which the transcript often gets wrong: [name as transcribed → correct name].

Then read and edit every set before it goes out. The notes go out under your name.
