AI task inventory

Worksheet 1 of 10, from Chapter 2 of the book.

List every task you do regularly. Fill in one row per task. Don’t borrow anyone else’s sort, including mine.

Task, in your own words Who used to do it What the AI does with it now What you still do, check or fix What doing it by hand teaches, or taught, you Your sort: AI takes it / AI helps / you keep it

Sample (part of my table from Chapter 2):

Task Where it landed What doing it by hand taught me
Weekly status deck for senior leadership AI takes it. I approve the final version and fix the formatting. To review the information closely and know what to look for
Weekly master status page AI helps. It pulls the information; I check every item. What’s worth reporting, and what’s a duplicate or noise
Meeting notes AI helps. It writes them; I read and edit every set. How to capture what was decided, who owns it, and when it’s due
Running the meeting I keep it. How to keep a meeting on track
One-on-one conversations with people on my projects I keep it. How to work with people and get them moving

Keep the inventory. You’ll use one task from it for the checks list.
